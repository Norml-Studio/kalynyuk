**Dominant approach: Divi Builder** (11 of 15 pages, 73%). The four "Divi + Gutenberg"
pages have a stray `<!-- wp:… -->` comment alongside Divi shortcodes — usually the
residue of a page that was started in Gutenberg and then switched to the Divi Builder.
The two Gutenberg-only pages (`Portugal`, `privacy-policy`) are also the two that aren't
in the menu, which reinforces that they're leftovers rather than a deliberate second
authoring track.

### Content duplication — the single biggest maintenance hazard

Two `et_pb_code` module bodies are **byte-identical** (verified by md5) across the
homepage and the calculator page:

| Module | Size | md5 (first 12) | On page 11 | On page 566 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Calculator CSS block (`/* ---- Your existing styles (kept intact) ---- */`) | 75,257 B | `23c2cf38ffe4` | slot 0 | slot 1 |
| Calculator layout CSS (`.item { gap: 12px; …`) | 22,186 B | `2a345770b16f` | slot 1 | slot 2 |

That is **~95 KB of CSS/JS maintained in two places with no shared source.** Changing the
calculator's styling requires editing both, or the two pages drift. It also means every
Divi save on either page writes another ~95 KB revision (see below).

The same block is *also* saved as Divi library layout `Calculator` (ID 863, 43 KB) with
its own `[gravityform id="3"]` — a third copy of the surrounding structure.

**If any consolidation work is authorised on this project, this is the first thing to
fix** — most naturally by moving the CSS into CodeKit post 1907 and deleting it from both
code modules.
